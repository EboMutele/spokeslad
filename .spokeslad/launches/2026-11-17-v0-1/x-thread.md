I built Spokeslad: a free Claude Code plugin that runs the launch and comms workflow big companies use, for devs with no comms team.

It drafts. You publish. It never posts for you.

https://github.com/EboMutele/spokeslad
---
How it works: you brief it once. Who it's for, what you want people to repeat, what you can prove, what you must never say.

Every other command checks against that file.
---
/spokeslad:listen watches HN, Reddit and GitHub and tells you what changed, why it matters and what to do.

/spokeslad:plan scores your news into tiers, so a bug fix doesn't get a launch and a launch doesn't get one post.
---
Every draft gets checked twice before you see it:

1. A linter that fails hype, unsourced claims and platform-rule breaks
2. A reviewer agent that never sees the conversation that wrote the drafts, and writes the first critical reply
---
Its first test was its own launch. The brief, competitor scan, plan, drafts and the red-team report that caught my mistakes are all in the repo.

It's v0.1. Feedback welcome, especially blunt feedback.
