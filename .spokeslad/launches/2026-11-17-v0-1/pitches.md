Hi, your 17 October guide to publishing a plugin to the Claude Directory covers
getting listed. I built a plugin for what comes after: telling people about it.

Spokeslad is a free, MIT-licensed Claude Code plugin for launch and comms prep. It
writes one messaging file from your repo, watches Hacker News, Reddit and GitHub,
sizes news into tiers, drafts each channel, and runs every draft through a linter and
a separate reviewer agent. It never posts on your behalf.

The repo is already public, including the files from its own launch prep. If it's
