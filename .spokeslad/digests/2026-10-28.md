## What changed

1. **A direct competitor is growing fast.** Newsjack (elvisun/newsjack, MIT) is an
   open-source PR skill library for Claude Code, Codex and others: newsjacking
   detection, journalist fit-checks, pitches, crisis holding statements and AI-answer
   visibility audits. About 1.7K stars per a listing verified 2026-10-25; a snapshot
   about a month after creation showed 365 stars added in 30 days.
   ([listing](https://www.opensourcealternatives.to/item/newsjack),
   [snapshot](https://www.sourcepulse.org/projects/31212896))
2. **Other PR plugins exist.** Claude-PR-Media-Work-Plugin (danielrosehill, MIT,
   7 stars) covers agency-style monitoring, pitching, crisis and reports
   ([repo](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-PR-Media-Work-Plugin)). Several
   single-skill PR and crisis packs are listed in skill directories.
3. **"PR" means pull request to our audience.** Buyer-style searches for PR tools for
   developer projects returned only pull-request and code-review tools.
4. **AI-answer baseline:** Spokeslad appears in 0 of 6 buyer questions (expected,
   it is unreleased). Newsjack appears in 2 of 6, opendirectory in 1 of 6.
5. **r/ClaudeAI routes showcases to a megathread.** Standalone posts need at least
   50 karma; otherwise use the Built with Claude showcase megathread and its Rule 7
   requirements.
   ([mod comment](https://redlib.groet-infra.nl/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1sly3jm/built_with_claude_project_showcase_megathread/oq1cg2n/?context=3))

## Why it matters

1. We cannot claim "first" or "only". Positioning must rest on what is different and
   checkable: developer channels, code-checked drafts, independent review, no posting.
2. Newsjack is journalist-first; Spokeslad is developer-community-first. That is a
   real, defensible difference, and it matches the original intent: reaching people
   through the internet rather than through journalists.
3. Our title and copy must say "launch" and "public relations", never "PR" alone.
4. The baseline gives the launch a measurable target.
5. Reddit plan must follow megathread rules or it will be removed.
